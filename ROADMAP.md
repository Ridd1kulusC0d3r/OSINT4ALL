## Long-term model

OSINT4ALL should become an **open intelligence resource graph**:

    Resource
      ↕
    Jurisdiction
      ↕
    Discipline
      ↕
    Use Case
      ↕
    Validation
      ↕
    Evidence / Provenance

The goal is not to win a contest for the longest README. The goal is to let an analyst find the right source, understand where it applies, assess its reliability and use it responsibly.
