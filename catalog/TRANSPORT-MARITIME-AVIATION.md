# Transport, Maritime & Aviation OSINT

Transport intelligence often supports GEOINT, conflict verification, logistics research, environmental investigations and chronology reconstruction.

## Coverage

### Aviation

- aircraft tracking;
- airport/route context;
- public registration records;
- historical movement where lawfully available;
- imagery and NOTAM/contextual sources.

Anchor: [OpenSky Network](https://opensky-network.org/).

### Maritime

- vessel identity and public movement context;
- fishing activity;
- port/route context;
- environmental and trade analysis.

Anchor: [Global Fishing Watch](https://globalfishingwatch.org/map/).

### Vehicles & surface transport

The Astrosp and jivoi reference ecosystems expose useful categories for VIN/vehicle research and transport records. OSINT4ALL will implement these primarily through **country/jurisdiction pages**, because registration and access rules vary substantially.

## Analytical caution

Tracking data can be incomplete, delayed, spoofed, filtered or unavailable. A map point is an observation from a data system, not proof of intent, ownership or activity.
