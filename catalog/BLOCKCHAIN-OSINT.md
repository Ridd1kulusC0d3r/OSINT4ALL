# Blockchain & Cryptocurrency OSINT

Public-ledger research is treated here as an OSINT/FININT specialization.

[K2SOsint Legendary Crypto](https://github.com/K2SOsint/Legendary_Crypto) is tracked as a curated upstream for public explorers, visualization, abuse/scam databases, APIs, AML/compliance and training references.

Useful public-source classes include blockchain explorers, transaction/address visualization, public scam reports, sanctions notices, public on-chain datasets and entity/relationship analysis.

Examples surfaced by the reviewed upstream include **Mempool.space, Etherscan, Blockchair, GraphSense, Chainabuse and official sanctions-search resources**.

## Evidence labels

- **Observed on-chain** — direct ledger observation.
- **Provider-labeled** — attribution supplied by a third party.
- **Corroborated** — independently supported attribution.
- **Inferred** — analyst conclusion.
- **Unresolved** — insufficient evidence.

OSINT4ALL does not provide guidance for concealing transfers, evading compliance controls or acquiring illicit assets.
