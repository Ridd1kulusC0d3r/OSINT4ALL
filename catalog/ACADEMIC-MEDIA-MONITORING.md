# Academic, Media & Monitoring OSINT

This catalog groups slower, evidence-rich research sources with faster monitoring surfaces.

## Academic & grey literature

Use academic databases, institutional repositories, conference material and technical reports to establish terminology, prior research and provenance.

## News & media

Treat media as sources with editorial context, not neutral databases. Record publisher, author, publication time, update history and primary-source links.

## Fact checking

Fact-checking resources can provide useful prior verification work, but analysts should inspect the underlying evidence for important claims.

## Monitoring

jivoi explicitly maintains OSINT blogs and RSS feeds; Astrosp includes news, analytics and monitoring-oriented categories. OSINT4ALL will expand this into a monitored-source layer:

    Feed/source
       ↓
    Change detection
       ↓
    Deduplication
       ↓
    Entity/topic tagging
       ↓
    Analyst triage
       ↓
    Evidence capture
       ↓
    Alert / intelligence product
