## Analyst workflow

1. **Define the identifier and legal basis.**
2. **Discover candidate profiles without assuming identity.**
3. **Compare stable attributes, chronology and cross-platform context.**
4. **Archive relevant public evidence with timestamps and provenance.**
5. **Separate observed facts from inference.**
6. **Corroborate before attribution.**

A username match is a lead, not an identity proof. The internet has spent decades reusing the same 40 usernames; analysts should not pretend otherwise.
