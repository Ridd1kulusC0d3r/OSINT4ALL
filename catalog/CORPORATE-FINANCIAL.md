# Corporate & Financial OSINT

Corporate and financial investigations combine official registries, ownership records, sanctions/public notices, procurement, litigation, trade and contextual open sources.

## Core questions

- Does the entity legally exist?
- In which jurisdiction?
- Who are the recorded officers/directors/beneficial owners where public?
- Which related entities share addresses, officers, domains or infrastructure?
- What public procurement, litigation or regulatory history exists?
- What claims are official records versus commercial aggregations or analyst inference?

## Current anchor resources

- [OpenCorporates](https://opencorporates.com/) — cross-jurisdiction company discovery.
- Country/federation pages in [countries/](../countries/README.md) — preferred route for official registries.
- [ICIJ Offshore Leaks](https://offshoreleaks.icij.org/) — investigative dataset reference surfaced in broader OSINT lists.

## Evidence rule

Commercial aggregators are discovery aids. Prefer the authoritative registry or primary record for material claims whenever available.
