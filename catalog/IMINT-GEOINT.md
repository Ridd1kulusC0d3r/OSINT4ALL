## Practical workflow

1. Preserve the original media and provenance.
2. Extract metadata without assuming it is authentic.
3. Inspect visual clues.
4. Perform reverse-image/video search.
5. Build candidate locations.
6. Compare against maps, street imagery and satellite imagery.
7. Use sun/shadow/weather/time clues where relevant.
8. Corroborate with independent sources.
9. Record confidence and unresolved alternatives.
