## Analytical safeguards

1. Prefer official and primary sources where available.
2. Record imagery date, provider, resolution and processing level.
3. Separate what is visible from what is inferred.
4. Do not infer intent from infrastructure change alone.
5. Corroborate with policy, procurement, scientific, trade and official information.
6. Treat social-media claims about radiation or nuclear events as unverified until supported by credible measurements and independent sources.
7. Use explicit confidence levels.
8. Avoid publishing details that create unnecessary physical-security risk.

## CBRNE scope expansion

Future safe coverage can include public health preparedness, treaty verification, regulatory datasets, incident databases, scientific publications, emergency-management resources and non-proliferation methodology. Operational weaponization content is outside scope.
