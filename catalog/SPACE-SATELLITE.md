## Analyst questions this category can answer

- Which public Earth-observation sources cover an area?
- What historical imagery exists?
- What changed between two dates?

## Limitation

High-resolution imagery, revisit rates, tasking and regional availability may be restricted by commercial, legal or geopolitical decisions. Record the source, capture date, resolution, processing level and access conditions whenever imagery supports a conclusion.
