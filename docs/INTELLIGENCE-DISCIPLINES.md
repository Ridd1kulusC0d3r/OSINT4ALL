# Intelligence Disciplines & OSINT Specializations
# Disciplinas de Inteligência e Especializações OSINT

> Working taxonomy for repository navigation. Different governments, institutions and research communities use overlapping definitions.

OSINT4ALL separates **collection disciplines**, **analytical domains** and **practitioner specializations**. A resource may map to several categories.

## Core collection disciplines

| Discipline | Meaning | Typical open-source overlap |
|---|---|---|
| **OSINT** | Open Source Intelligence | Public records, web, media, datasets, research |
| **HUMINT** | Human Intelligence | Public interviews, witness material and human-source context; sensitive/private collection is outside this repo |
| **SIGINT** | Signals Intelligence | Public spectrum research, technical publications and openly available telemetry |
| **COMINT** | Communications Intelligence | Public communications research and openly published communications datasets |
| **ELINT** | Electronic Intelligence | Radar/emitter research using public technical and geospatial sources |
| **FISINT** | Foreign Instrumentation Signals Intelligence | Public research on telemetry/instrumentation signatures |
| **GEOINT** | Geospatial Intelligence | Maps, satellite imagery, terrain, geolocation and change detection |
| **IMINT** | Imagery Intelligence | Image/video interpretation, metadata and visual verification |
| **MASINT** | Measurement and Signature Intelligence | Public sensor datasets, spectral/physical signatures and scientific measurements |

## Technical and domain intelligence

| Discipline / domain | Focus |
|---|---|
| **TECHINT** | Technical characteristics, capabilities, components and systems |
| **CYBINT** | Intelligence derived from cyber and digital ecosystems |
| **CTI** | Cyber Threat Intelligence: actors, campaigns, infrastructure, malware, TTPs and IoCs |
| **MEDINT** | Medical and health-related intelligence from appropriate lawful/public sources |
| **FININT** | Financial intelligence, corporate ownership, sanctions, transactions and financial networks |
| **DOMEX** | Document and media exploitation |
| **WEBINT** | Web-derived intelligence, including websites, search engines and online content |
| **DNINT** | Digital Network Intelligence; usage varies across institutions |
| **SOCMINT** | Social Media Intelligence |
| **Identity Intelligence** | Identity resolution and entity correlation |
| **Corporate / Business Intelligence** | Companies, ownership, filings, directors, counterparties and market context |
| **Supply-Chain Intelligence** | Suppliers, logistics, dependencies, trade and manufacturing relationships |
| **Infrastructure Intelligence** | Physical and digital infrastructure mapping |
| **Dark-Web / Underground Intelligence** | Lawful research into underground ecosystems |
| **Threat Intelligence** | Broader threat-focused intelligence across cyber, physical, fraud and geopolitical risks |

## Physical-world and situational specializations

- **Satellite / Space OSINT** — TLEs, orbital tracking, Earth observation and space situational awareness.
- **Aviation Intelligence** — aircraft registries, ADS-B, airports, routes and aviation infrastructure.
- **Maritime Intelligence** — AIS, ship registries, ports, fishing and maritime activity.
- **Environmental Intelligence** — deforestation, fires, land use, pollution, wildlife and environmental crime.
- **Climate / Weather Intelligence** — meteorological and climate datasets used for verification and context.
- **Conflict OSINT** — geolocation, chronology, verification and situational awareness in armed conflict.
- **CBRNE Intelligence** — chemical, biological, radiological, nuclear and explosive threat research.
- **Nuclear OSINT** — public-source analysis of nuclear facilities, policy, proliferation, imagery and monitoring.
- **Critical Infrastructure / OT Intelligence** — defensive visibility into ICS/SCADA/OT ecosystems and public exposure.

## Investigative specializations

- **People OSINT** — names, aliases, usernames, email, phone, public records and relationship mapping.
- **SOCMINT** — social profiles, posts, networks, communities, narratives and platform-specific research.
- **Corporate OSINT** — legal entities, ownership, directors, procurement, litigation and corporate networks.
- **Fraud / KYC / AML OSINT** — entity verification, sanctions, adverse media and suspicious relationship discovery.
- **Geolocation & Chronolocation** — determining where and when media/events occurred.
- **Image / Video Verification** — authenticity, provenance, reverse search, frame analysis and metadata.
- **Cyber Infrastructure OSINT** — domains, IPs, certificates, exposed services and internet infrastructure.
- **Code / Repository Intelligence** — public source code, commits, dependency context and exposed artifacts.
- **Breach / Exposure Intelligence** — public reporting and lawful exposure datasets; credential abuse is out of scope.
- **Disinformation / Narrative Intelligence** — information operations, narratives, amplification and source tracing.
- **Elections / Civic OSINT** — official electoral data, campaign finance, public statements and civic records.
- **Legal / Court OSINT** — court decisions, dockets, statutes, regulatory and enforcement records.
- **Academic / Scientific OSINT** — papers, patents, grants, institutions, datasets and researchers.
- **Archive Intelligence** — web archives, historical documents and change-over-time analysis.
- **Sanctions Intelligence** — sanctions lists, enforcement actions, ownership/control relationships.
- **Trade Intelligence** — imports, exports, customs, shipping and commodity flows.
- **Real-Estate / Land Intelligence** — land registries, cadastre, property and zoning data.
- **Procurement Intelligence** — tenders, contracts, suppliers and public expenditure.
- **Media Intelligence** — news ecosystems, source provenance and narrative tracking.
- **Patent / Innovation Intelligence** — patents, inventors, assignees and technology trends.
- **Academic Network Intelligence** — institutions, labs, grants, researchers and publication networks.

## Cross-domain examples

Sentinel / Copernicus can map to GEOINT, IMINT, Environmental Intelligence, Conflict OSINT and Satellite OSINT.

MISP can map to CTI, CYBINT, Threat Intelligence and IoC sharing.

Maltego can support People OSINT, Corporate OSINT, CTI, Identity Intelligence and relationship analysis.
