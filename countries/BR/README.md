# 🇧🇷 Brazil / Brasil

**Status:** 🟡 Initial AI-assisted profile. Human validation required.

Brazil is the first detailed jurisdiction in OSINT4ALL because it combines a federal structure, extensive public-data ecosystems and strong regional variation.

- States / Estados (UF)
- Federal District / Distrito Federal

## Planned subnational coverage

All 26 states plus the Federal District should have their own pages using ISO 3166-2 codes, for example BR-MG, BR-SP, BR-RJ and BR-DF.

Each UF page should eventually cover judiciary, transparency, companies, land/property, procurement, police/public safety datasets where legally appropriate, environmental sources, transport, archives and local open-data portals.

## Legal/ethical note

Brazilian OSINT involving personal data should be assessed under the applicable legal context, including data-protection, secrecy, procedural and platform rules. Public availability alone does not eliminate privacy or proportionality concerns.
