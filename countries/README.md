# Country & Federation Index / Índice por País e Federação

OSINT4ALL uses a **jurisdiction-first** model because the usefulness and legality of a source often depend on where the investigation occurs.

## Structure

    countries/<ISO2>/
    ├── README.md
    ├── federal.md
    ├── subnational/
    └── sources/

## Country page fields

Each country page should eventually contain:

- national/federal public sources;
- states, provinces, regions or equivalent subdivisions;
- municipalities/local authorities;
- courts and legal records;
- companies and ownership;
- land/property/cadastre;
- procurement and spending;
- sanctions and regulatory data;
- transport and mobility;
- elections and political finance;
- environmental data;
- geospatial and satellite sources;
- cyber/digital infrastructure;
- archives and media;
- privacy/data-protection notes;
- local-language search terms.

## Federation-aware model

For federal countries, pages should explicitly distinguish **national/federal**, **state/province/canton/Land**, and **municipal/local** sources.

For unitary countries, the same structure maps national, regional and local authorities without inventing a federation that does not exist.
